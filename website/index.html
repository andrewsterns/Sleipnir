Questions

Does Pro include AI usage?
No. You use your own Claude, Gemini or OpenAI key, or a free local model with Ollama, so you only pay your provider for what you use.
What counts as commercial use?
Using Sleipnir for paid work or inside a business. Personal projects, learning and open-source hobby work are free.
How many computers can I use it on?
Up to 3 of your own. Remove the key from one in Settings ▸ License to move it to another.
Is "Founder" pricing going away?
Yes. It's for early supporters. Later licenses may cost more or be subscriptions; founder keys stay lifetime.
