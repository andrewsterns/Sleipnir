Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-26

Sleipnir is a local desktop app. There is no Sleipnir account and no Sleipnir server your data passes through. No analytics, telemetry, tracking or ads.

What can leave your computer

  • AI requests, only if you connect a cloud AI provider with your own key. Prompts and the context you include go directly to that provider under its privacy policy. With a local Ollama model, nothing leaves your machine.
  • Update checks to GitHub Releases, a normal web request.
  • License checks to Lemon Squeezy, only if you enter a Pro key: the key and your computer's name, at activation and about once a week after. The free version never makes this call.

Purchases

When you buy Sleipnir Pro, Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email and payment details to process the order, under its privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details from Lemon Squeezy to provide your license and support. We don't sell or share them.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and asks GitHub's API for the latest download links; both receive standard request data such as your IP address.

The full in-app policy is in PRIVACY.md.
