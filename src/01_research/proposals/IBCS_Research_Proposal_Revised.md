# IBCS Research Proposal (Revised)

## Summary
This document outlines the goals, methodology, and expected outcomes for the IBCS research initiative. The proposal is focused on advancing user-centered design, prototyping, and research practices to inform the development of effective solutions for target users.

## Primary Personas
This research and prototype effort is focused on the following key user personas:

- **Sergeant Cypher (E-5, 25D) – Cyber Network Defender**
- **Specialist Ramirez (E-3, 14E) – Patriot Fire Control Enhanced Operator / Maintainer**

See `/research/personas/` for detailed persona documentation.

## Deliverable Goals
- ✧ Listener to track time
- ✧ Parameters for difficulty
- ✦ Two users can interact with the same mission at a time
- ✦ Simulate a mission: a map with tracks on it
- ✦ Tracks that move; user can fire at them
- ✦ Backend generates speed, IFF response, and engagement with an interceptor
  - User can defend
- ✦ Launcher reloading
- ✦ Sensor degradation that corresponds with alerts

## Objectives
- Define clear research questions and hypotheses
- Identify user needs and pain points
- Develop and test prototypes based on research findings
- Gather and analyze user feedback to iterate on designs

## Methodology
- Conduct user interviews and surveys to collect qualitative and quantitative data
- Analyze user demographics and behaviors
- Create personas and journey maps to guide design
- Develop low- and high-fidelity prototypes
- Test prototypes with users and collect actionable feedback

## Expected Outcomes
- Comprehensive understanding of user needs
- Validated prototypes ready for further development
- Actionable insights for design and product teams
- Documentation of research process and findings for future reference

## Relevance to Prototype, Research, and Design Goals
This proposal provides a structured approach to integrating research into the design and prototyping process. It ensures that user needs drive decision-making and that prototypes are validated through real user feedback, supporting the overall goals of user-centered design and continuous improvement.
