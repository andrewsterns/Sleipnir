# Persona: E-3 (14E) - Patriot Fire Control Enhanced Operator / Maintainer

**Name:** Specialist Ramirez  
**Quote:** “When we have issues with the equipment, it falls on me to figure out what is wrong…”

---

**Age:** 20 Years  
**Education:** High School diploma  
**Occupation:** Air Threat Defender  
**Location:** Deployed

## Duties and Responsibilities
As a Patriot Fire Control Enhanced Operator / Maintainer, Spc. Ramirez is responsible for operating and maintaining the five major parts of the Patriot Fire Control system. He acts quickly and with precision to identify, track, and engage those identified threats using one of the world’s most advanced interceptor missile systems capable of destroying enemy air threats at a moment’s notice.

## Wants & Needs
- My system is critical and must be as resilient as possible
- I don't want to be a cyber defender, but I want their help if I am attacked
- I need a simple way to be notified that I am under cyber attack

## Frustrations
- Troubleshooting some of our typical problems is time consuming
- Can’t tell the difference between a connection issue and a cyber attack
- Already goes through a lot of training and procedures in current job
